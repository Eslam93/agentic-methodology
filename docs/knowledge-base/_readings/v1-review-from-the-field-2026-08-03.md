## What it said

The reviewer was a harness that had already been in daily use for weeks on a multi-repository
estate. Its summary of v1:

- **Most of v1's flow the installation already had**, arrived at from practice rather than from
  the documents.
- **Two mechanisms worth stealing regardless:** the evidence ladder, which ranks what settles a
  finding, and the direction delta, which reports what shipped versus what was asked as a separate
  signature from correctness.
- **Honest weaknesses:** v1 was heavy; it depended on two vendors; it presumed a competent
  operator; it had no outcome evidence that any of it improved results.

## What v2 does with it

| Judgement | v2 |
|---|---|
| the evidence ladder is worth keeping | kept as five lines in standing orders and in the review skill (V-04) |
| the direction delta is worth keeping | kept as one line in the `/work` hand-back |
| heavy | the documentation layer is deleted; rule and skill budgets are enforced (D-01, D-03) |
| two vendors | the default reviewer is a fresh subagent; Codex is optional (D-05) |
| presumes a competent operator | `/orient` reports and stops; `/work` asks only what changes the build; the stop-list pauses the build on the dangerous cases |
| no outcome evidence | still true. Recorded as the first line of root cause B in `what-we-do-not-know.md` |
